# Value-Over-Price Optimization

## Definition
Positioning long-term experience and total cost of ownership against the industry default of lowest upfront cost.

## The Math
Upfront price is a single data point. Total cost of ownership includes:
- Service reliability
- Time spent troubleshooting
- Stress of hidden fees
- Long-term performance degradation

## How to Frame It
"The best value isn't always the lowest price—it's the option that gives you the best long-term experience."
Map the first-year savings (typically 25–30%). Show the 3-year and 5-year projections. Let the system prove the ROI.

## Why It Works
It aligns with the Guarded Seeker's desire for certainty. It shifts the conversation from "Can I afford it?" to "Can I afford not to have it work correctly?"
