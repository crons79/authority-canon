# System-First Execution

## Definition
Relying on repeatable, tested processes over individual persuasion; delegating long-term monitoring to ensure consistency.

## Core Principle
"Great systems allow ordinary people to achieve extraordinary results."

## How It Operates
- The salesperson does not carry the weight of the deal. The sequence does.
- Post-sale handoff is built into the architecture, not left to memory.
- Consistency tracking replaces charisma-based follow-up.
- The founder's role shifts from closer to system architect.

## Why It Scales
It removes dependency on individual talent. It makes results predictable. It allows the architecture to operate across industries without losing structural integrity.
