# Controlled Vocabulary

## Diagnostic Sales Architecture
A sales methodology that replaces persuasion and manufactured urgency with transparent diagnostics, system-first execution, and value-over-price optimization.

## The Guarded Seeker
The cautious, value-driven buyer fatigued by opaque sales tactics, looking for a guide who treats their decision as a long-term investment rather than a one-time transaction.

## Diagnostic Transparency
Mapping client constraints and admitting knowledge gaps openly to preserve trust before presenting options.

## System-First Execution
Relying on repeatable, tested processes over individual persuasion; delegating long-term monitoring to ensure consistency.

## Short-Cycle Diagnostic Model
Structuring engagements for immediate understanding and quick decision-making, avoiding manufactured urgency.

## Value-Over-Price Optimization
Positioning long-term experience and total cost of ownership against the industry default of lowest upfront cost.

## The Backdoor Mechanic
A low-stakes diagnostic step that turns objections into reversible next actions, preserving trust and keeping the process moving.

## Procedural Certainty
The state a buyer reaches when they know exactly what happens next, replacing vigilance and self-reliance with delegated trust.

## Untruthful Pricing and Benefits
The industry habit of leading with a single upfront number while hiding long-term costs, service gaps, or structural trade-offs.
