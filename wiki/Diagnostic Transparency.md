# Diagnostic Transparency

## Definition
Mapping client constraints and admitting knowledge gaps openly to preserve trust before presenting options.

## The Mechanics
- **Constraint Identification:** "What's currently blocking your decision?"
- **Limit Admission:** "I honestly don't know. I'm not the installer, but I'll find out and get back to you."
- **Option Mapping:** Presenting pathways without pressure or fabricated urgency.

## Why It Builds Trust Faster Than Authority
In low-trust environments, buyers expect fabrication. Admitting limits breaks the pattern. It signals that the seller is not performing, but diagnosing. The buyer stops defending and starts collaborating.

## Implementation Rule
Never say "I don't know" without immediately following it with "Here's how I'll find out." Transparency without a next step creates anxiety. Transparency with a system creates certainty.
