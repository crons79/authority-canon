# Framework: Short-Cycle Diagnostic Model

## Definition
Structuring engagements for immediate understanding and quick decision-making, avoiding manufactured urgency.

## Components
1. Targeted outreach
2. Assistant intake
3. Pre-call prep
4. Diagnosis/education
5. Transparent presentation
6. Backdoor mechanic
7. Follow-up

## Application
The 7-step sequence that compresses the sales cycle while increasing decision confidence.

## What It Replaces
Long sales cycles, follow-up fatigue, and artificial deadline pressure.

## Downstream Use
All expression agents. This is the operational spine of the category.
