# Case Studies & Outcome Evidence

## Excel System Optimization
**Context:** Inefficient tracking across a multi-year operational workflow.
**Intervention:** Created a centralized spreadsheet system storing all required information.
**Outcome:** Saved time, reduced repetitive work, streamlined the process. The system was used by the company for five years post-implementation.
**Proof of System-First Execution:** The architecture operated independently of individual involvement.

## Window Replacement Transparency
**Context:** High-stakes home improvement decision with opaque contractor pricing.
**Intervention:** Admitted lack of installation knowledge, outlined process honestly, mapped constraints transparently.
**Outcome:** Preserved trust through limit admission. Client proceeded with full confidence.
**Proof of Diagnostic Transparency:** "I honestly don't know. I'm not the installer..." became a trust catalyst, not a weakness.
