# Framework: Value-Over-Price Optimization

## Definition
Positioning long-term experience and total cost of ownership against the industry default of lowest upfront cost.

## Components
- Total cost mapping
- Experience valuation
- First-year savings documentation
- Long-term ROI framing

## Application
Used during presentation and objection handling to reframe price as an investment in certainty and reduced friction.

## What It Replaces
The race to the bottom on upfront price and untruthful pricing and benefits.
