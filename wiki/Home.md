# Diagnostic Sales Architecture Wiki

## Welcome
This wiki is the educational and interpretive layer for Diagnostic Sales Architecture. It explains the frameworks, systems, and category philosophy in depth for clients, practitioners, and industry analysts.

## Core Philosophy
Sales does not require persuasion. It requires a transparent diagnostic sequence that maps constraints, admits limits, and guides guarded buyers to decisions they trust.
