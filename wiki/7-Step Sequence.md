# The 7-Step Sequence

## Overview
The operational spine of Diagnostic Sales Architecture. It compresses the sales cycle while increasing decision confidence through structured transparency.

## The Steps
1. **Targeted Outreach** — Direct, value-first contact aligned with the Guarded Seeker's research habits
2. **Assistant Intake** — Low-friction qualification and constraint gathering
3. **Pre-Call Prep** — System-driven mapping of client context before engagement
4. **Diagnosis/Education** — Constraint identification, limit admission, option mapping
5. **Transparent Presentation** — Clear, unpressured delivery of total cost and long-term value
6. **The Backdoor** — Reversible diagnostic step to handle hesitation without pressure
7. **Follow-Up** — System-tracked stewardship, not chasing

## Why It Works
It removes the friction of uncertainty. Each step builds procedural certainty. The buyer never feels sold to; they feel guided. The seller never relies on charisma; they rely on the sequence.
