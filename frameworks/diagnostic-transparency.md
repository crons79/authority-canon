# Framework: Diagnostic Transparency

## Definition
Mapping client constraints and admitting knowledge gaps openly to preserve trust before presenting options.

## Components
- Constraint identification
- Limit admission ("I don't know")
- Option mapping without pressure

## Application
Used during the discovery and education phases to establish trust before any presentation.

## What It Replaces
The expectation that the salesperson must have every answer immediately and fabricate authority.
