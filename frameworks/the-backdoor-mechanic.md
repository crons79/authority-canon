# Framework: The Backdoor Mechanic

## Definition
A low-stakes diagnostic step that turns objections into reversible next actions, preserving trust and keeping the process moving.

## Components
- Objection acknowledgment
- Soft pivot
- Reversible step ("Let's just run the numbers and see")
- No-pressure framing

## Application
Used when a client hesitates or raises a barrier. It replaces hard closing with a diagnostic detour.

## What It Replaces
High-pressure closing tactics and the expectation of an immediate "yes" or "no."
